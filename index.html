José Manuel Ferreira Machado
Full Professor & Scientific Researcher in Knowledge Engineering, Health Informatics and Artificial Intelligence — University of Minho, School of Engineering.
ALGORITMI & LASI Dept. Informatics PhD 2002 · Habilitation 2011
Biography

At the University of Minho since 1988, José Machado teaches and researches across Databases, Artificial Intelligence, Knowledge Engineering and Medical Informatics.

He coordinates the Computer Science and Technology research line and the Knowledge Engineering Lab, sits on the coordination board of LASI — the largest Portuguese Associate Laboratory, integrating 13 research centres — and is a member of the Academic Senate.

He is one of the authors of the AIDA platform (Agency for Interoperation, Dissemination and Medical Information Archives), in operation across several Portuguese hospitals and recognised with national awards including Hospital of the Future, Good Practices in Health and the Portugal Digital Awards.

He co-authored the first version of the European Diploma Supplement, directed the ALGORITMI R&D Center (2017–2024) and led the European project DRIVES, bringing together 23 institutions across 11 countries. He has chaired four international conferences and the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society, Portuguese Chapter.
